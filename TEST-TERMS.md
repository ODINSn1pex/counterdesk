# Counterdesk – Test version terms and privacy notice

**Version 1.0.0-beta.2**

## 1. What this is
Counterdesk is a customer service program for Shopify stores. This is a pre-release test version.
It is free of charge and meant for testing.

## 2. Use
You may download, install and use this test version in your own business, also with your live Shopify store.
You may share the unmodified download link. You may not sell, rent out or distribute modified copies.

## 3. No warranty
The test version is provided as is. It may contain errors and its features may change.
Please check important actions (e.g. changed customer details) in the Shopify admin as well.
As this is a free test version, liability is limited to intent and gross negligence. This does not limit
liability for injury to life, body or health, or liability that cannot be excluded by law.

## 4. Data and privacy
- Counterdesk has no server. The program runs on your PC and exchanges data only with your Shopify store
  (Shopify's own terms and privacy policy apply there).
- Credentials, settings and the log file are stored on your PC (%USERPROFILE%\.counterdesk.env, %APPDATA%\Counterdesk).
- Customer cases are stored on your PC or, in team mode, in your Shopify store as data of your own Shopify app.
- On start, Counterdesk asks GitHub (api.github.com) whether a newer version exists. GitHub receives your IP address
  and the program name, no customer or store data.
- Links to your store and the Shopify admin open in the browser chosen under Settings > Links (default: your system browser).
- The publisher of Counterdesk does not receive any of your data or your customers' data.
  You remain responsible for your customers' data under data protection law.
- Feedback on GitHub (Issues) is public. Please never post customer data, screenshots with customer data or credentials there.
